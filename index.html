01 — Research

Why look beyond general relativity?

My goal is to understand the gravitational behaviour and observational signatures of ultra-compact objects — black holes and compact stars, with and without horizons. Near them, higher-curvature effects peak, so any departure from Einstein's theory should be magnified rather than hidden as in weak fields.

General relativity is an approximation: it lacks a consistent quantum completion and may pick up corrections near merging black holes. Gravitational-wave and shadow observations now let us test this directly. I combine theoretical analysis with observational constraints to pin down — or rule out — modified theories of gravity in the strong-field regime.
